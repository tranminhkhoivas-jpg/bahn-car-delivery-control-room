WORKFLOW: Vehicle problems. Warning lights, breakdowns, damage, flat tyres, fuel or charge trouble, safety stops.
1. safety: first driver message asks whether they are safe and stopped in a safe place, unless the conversation already has that answer (for example a reply to the 112 message); then use it and ask only what is still missing. Danger: create_employee_task priority 'top'.
2. facts_photo: exact location, what the dashboard shows (ask for a photo), whether the car can still move. Give no mechanical advice and never say it may be driven.
3. solution_question: the buyer decides how the car is handled. First plan draft = only a buyer draft (customer_message) asking how they want it solved (their own roadside assistance, a tow arranged by Bahn, or something else) plus {"kind":"buyer_decision","detail":"How the buyer wants the car recovered"}. No driver instruction, no booking, no cost yet.
4. staff_check: after the buyer's answer (follow-up), create_employee_task priority 'normal' with ask_time 'ready': task "The buyer chose: <their answer>. If you agree, give the time the car will be ready (or leave it not known); for a recovery Bahn arranges, write its price in the note. If you disagree, write in the note what the buyer should be told instead." This also applies when the buyer uses their own roadside assistance. Recovery, towing and repair are never booked by you.
5. buyer_revise: if the employee disagreed (their note says what to tell the buyer), file a plan draft with a customer_message written from that note in Bahn's voice plus {"kind":"buyer_decision","detail":"..."}. The employee approves that draft before it is sent; the buyer's new answer goes back to staff_check.
6. journey_options: only once the employee agreed AND gave a ready time: call journey_disruption with from_time = the 'plan from' time in the follow-up (the ready time plus the safety margin). Ready time not known yet: do not plan or rebook; check-ins ask staff again automatically and wake you.
7. plan: one draft with {"kind":"approve_cost","amount_eur":<employee's price>,"detail":"recovery, price from employee task #<id>"} (only when Bahn arranges recovery), the journey lines, a buyer draft, a delivery-contact draft when the time changes, and a driver note.
