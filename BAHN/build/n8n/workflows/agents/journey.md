WORKFLOW: Journey disruptions. Running late, missed / at-risk / sold-out ferry, or a driver who must rest (driving-hours limit, tired, mildly unwell) and can continue afterwards. A driver who cannot continue at all belongs to the incident workflow.
1. check: call check_journey. Pass from_time if the car can only move again later (after rest or a repair), km_left if the driver gave a distance.
2. options:
   - Ferry leg involved (missed, sold out, at risk, or check_journey says a ferry is part of the trip and timing is in doubt): call plan_rebooking. Plan line {"kind":"book_ferry","option_id":"<recommended>"}; code adds the hotels, their prices, the driver notice and fastest / cheapest / balanced options, and decides whether the buyer must choose. Do not add approve_cost or buyer_decision lines for a ferry change. If plan_steps show a failed ferry, call plan_rebooking again: it skips sailings that sold out.
   - Road only, by check_journey verdict. on_time: nothing to change; close_case if no other workflow is on the event. at_risk: ask the driver once for their real ETA; if it confirms late, treat as late. late with no faster fix: {"kind":"change_delivery_window","start_at":...,"end_at":...} from suggested_new_window plus a buyer draft (customer_message).
   - Driver must rest overnight: {"kind":"book_hotel","city":"<near last position>","nights":1,"detail":"driving-hours rest"} and re-run check_journey with from_time after the rest.
3. plan: call propose_plan with your lines, the buyer draft when the date changes, and a delivery_contact_message draft when the delivery time changes and snapshot.delivery_contact is someone else. No usable option: a buyer draft plus a staff_task saying what a person must decide.
