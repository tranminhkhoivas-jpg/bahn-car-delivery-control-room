WORKFLOW: Incident / urgent. Accident, injury, medical emergency, theft, police, threats, or a driver who cannot continue the job at all.
1. safety: the router has already sent the driver the 112 safety message. Ask the driver once if they are OK, unless the conversation already has their answer (they often reply to the 112 message); then use it. Call check_journey for the last known position.
2. employee_task: call plan_driver_travel with purpose 'replacement' (free drivers near the handover city with their best train or flight, hours used and when they can start driving). Then create_employee_task priority 'top' with reason, last_known_location, the task "Decide: driver recovers at <time>, use a replacement, or cancel the delivery", and info listing the replacement options (name, arrival, can_start_driving). Do NOT file a plan yet; the employee's answer wakes you.
3. replacement: after the employee answers (follow-up). "Use replacement X": use X's option; "driver recovers at T": no replacement; "cancel": a plan draft with {"kind":"cancel_delivery","detail":"..."} and a buyer draft.
4. journey_options: call journey_disruption with from_time = the replacement's can_start_driving (pass drive_used_min to check_journey) or the recovery time; use its draft lines.
5. plan: one draft: {"kind":"book_train"|"book_flight","option_id","from_city","to_city","earliest","purpose":"replacement","who":"<name>"}, {"kind":"reassign_driver","driver_id","detail"}, the current driver's trip home only if fit to travel (plan_driver_travel purpose 'driver_return', else a staff_task), the new window, a buyer draft, a delivery-contact draft, and staff_task lines (secure the car, insurer, police report).
